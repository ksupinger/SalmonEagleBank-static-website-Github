Financial Sovereignty for Indigenous Nations

SalmonEagleBank is envisioned as a crypto-friendly sovereign bank serving all tribes of Turtle Island (Kanatan), with global multi-currency support and modern digital banking services.

  • ◇

    Crypto-friendly banking

    Make room for digital assets alongside the currencies communities and businesses use every day.

  • ↔

    Global multi-currency support

    Help Indigenous businesses and communities manage more than one currency as they trade across borders.

  • ◎

    Digital banking

    Give communities a way to manage day-to-day finances online, wherever they live and work.
