OUR EXPERTISE

From circuit to code.
Expertise that connects.

We bring hardware, firmware, and interfaces together around your custom controller, backed by over 40 years of experience. We’ve worked on hundreds of products. We design enclosures in Fusion360, 3D print them in liquid resin, and create industrial designs for manufacturing. No design too small or too big.

  1. 01 / CIRCUIT DESIGN

    Fusion360 circuit design

    Custom circuit design in Fusion360, shaped around your application and electronic controller requirements.

  2. 02 / EMBEDDED SOFTWARE

    Embedded C++ firmware

    Firmware in C++ to bring your controller’s hardware, inputs, outputs, and operating logic together.

  3. 03 / PROCESSING

    The right processor

    Processor selection using ESP32 or Atmel Cortex M3 to suit your application’s requirements.

  4. 04 / COMMUNICATION

    CAN bus integration

    CAN bus for automotive and marine electronics, connecting controllers and devices within your system.

  5. 05 / HUMAN INTERFACE

    Color touch displays

    Color touch displays that give users a clear way to view information and interact with your controller.

  6. 06 / SENSING & CONTROL

    Sensors & actuators

    A wide selection of sensors and actuators to connect your controller to the physical world.

  7. 07 / 3D DESIGN

    3D design and manufacturing

    We use Fusion360 to design enclosures and parts for 3D printing and manufacturing.

  8. 08 / SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

    Cloud, desktop, and mobile apps

    We develop software for Microsoft Azure and build desktop and phone apps.

  9. 09 / AI SOFTWARE DEVELOPMENT

    AI software development

    We engineer AI software that captures how repetitive business tasks are performed and helps automate them.
